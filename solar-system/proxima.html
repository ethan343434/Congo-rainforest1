      Mouse wheel zooms the camera. The mouse pushes a virtual stick that slowly re-centres. The engines spool up to 0.9 c in open space and the pulse drive goes faster still; both slow themselves near planets. The pulse drive works anywhere, even straight off the ground: it skims over terrain and stops just above it if you dive in. At 0.9 c your clock runs 2.3× slower than the universe. With flight assist on, entries are held to a speed the hull survives (it still glows); with assist off (Z) you can burn up. Gravity is real everywhere: on Jupiter the hover thrusters lose and you need Shift to climb.

      PROXIMA B

      You fell through the black hole and came out 4.24 light-years from home, above a world circling the red dwarf Proxima Centauri.

      • Proxima b always shows its star the same face: scorched day side, frozen night side, and a twilight ring of life between them.
      • The air is breathable. Land, step outside (E) and explore.
      • Find supply crates. They hold a laser rifle (left click to fire) and energy cells.
      • Grazers and sky jellies are friendly. Dune claws on the day side and night stalkers in the dark are not.
